Case study 01

One campaign, two brands

Brief
The same promotions had to launch on two sister platforms, 567Rio and 123Rio, without either one looking like a copy of the other.
What I did
Kept the layout and message hierarchy, then rebuilt each brand's world: green and gold vs. cream and deep teal, Brazil-jersey mascots vs. carnival mascots, and each logo lockup.
Result
Matching sets the team can launch side by side, with the same mascot kept for each promo across every format.
